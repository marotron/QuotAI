Fair-share bars + trajectory

Bars on elapsed %. Default δ vs diagonal maps the trajectory over-triangle (0,0)–(0,100)–(100,100) into the upper half and the under-triangle (0,0)–(100,0)–(100,100) into the lower half (±1 = full triangle height at that elapsed). Also try burnRatio: (usage_b − usage_a) / fairShare (1× = exact fair).

Triangle map squeezes early over/under (tall slice) and stretches late over (short slice to 100%). burnRatio remains the per-slot intensity encoding. Legacy Δ / Absolute pills stay for comparison.

Variants

Two independent axes: layout (A/B/C, bottom bar or ←/→) and bar encoding (pills next to H).

Bar encoding

ModeWhat you seeHow under-burn appears
δ vs diagonal (default) Over △ → upper half, under △ → lower half. Mid-0 = on ideal. ±1 = full triangle at that elapsed. Blue = below diagonal (normalized); orange = above.
burnRatio Bar = (usage_b − usage_a) / fairShare. Mid-1 = exact fair for H. 1d → dayΔ% / quotaPerDay%. Blue = ratio < 1 (burned less than fair); orange = ratio > 1.
Δ vs fair Bar = burn − fairShare (legacy per-slot). Unused fair even while ahead of ideal.
Absolute + under gap Bar height = burn from 0; ghost = fair allowance. Blue fill from burn up to fair.
